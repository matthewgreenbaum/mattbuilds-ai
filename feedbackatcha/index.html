Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about FeedbackAtcha

What is FeedbackAtcha?

FeedbackAtcha is an AI-powered creative feedback coach. It helps you write clear, actionable feedback on marketing creative — ads, emails, decks, landing pages, and more — in minutes.

Who is this for?

B2B marketers who need to respond quickly and constructively to creative work from their team, agency, or vendor without defaulting to "looks great!" or vague critiques.

What types of creative can I review?

Ads, content (eBooks, blogs), copy, video, landing pages, emails, and decks. You can upload files, paste links, or describe what you received.

What feedback models are available?

Direct (COIN) — Context, Observation, Impact, Next Step. Great for structured feedback.
Diplomatic (SBI) — Situation, Behavior, Impact. Better for sensitive situations.
Coaching — Question-led, developmental tone. Ideal when you want to guide rather than direct.

Is my data stored or shared?

Your review sessions are saved locally in your browser. No data is stored on any server beyond the AI conversation itself.

Can I come back and continue a review?

Yes! Your sessions are saved in the sidebar. Click any entry to return to it.

How It Works

FeedbackAtcha walks you through a structured four-phase conversation. Each phase builds context so the final feedback is specific, fair, and actionable.
